MOD: Smart AI
ID:   smart_ai
STATUS: active code, ACTIVE (listed in scripts/active_mods.txt).

OWNERSHIP (2026-09-21 fix): units and buildings are matched to an AI
house by House identity (owner == aiHouse), never by GetName() string
(GetName returns the shared country/type ID, so same-country houses
collide). aiHouses excludes human, player-allied, and neutral houses
(Neutral/Special/Civilian via util.is_neutral_house).

What it does (scripts/mods/smart_ai/main.lua, ~230 lines; mod.json:
"Dynamic base flank defense and threat response"):

   1. FLANK-BREACH RALLY (proximity trigger): for each AI house
      (identity-matched, see OWNERSHIP above), an own building with a
      hostile combat unit inside 10 cells is a breach; idle reserve
      combat units far from the breach (> 6 cells) get a clean MoveTo
      toward it (NO trailing Hunt — Hunt overwrote the queued Move in
      the single-slot mission queue per OpenTS/YRpp, so reserves
      hunted from place; fixed 2026-09-28). No per-unit change
      detection — while a breach persists the orders re-issue every
      scan.
  2. CAPTURE-AWARE VALUABLES GUARD (MVP): high-value own units (APOC,
      HTNK) that are idle, lone (> 4 cells from own centroid) and within
      9 cells of an observable mind-control threat (MIND live-observed;
      YURIPR/YURI rules/wiki IDs) get one MoveTo toward the own group
      (transition-only + 600-frame refresh; engaged units never yanked).
      Owner flips are logged as observations only and drive no decisions.
  3. COMMANDER (base) + OFFICER (units) — classic split, same
      30-frame scan, no extra World calls; ID-ordered, no RNG/clock.
      Commander: breach rally (item 1) + garrison directives below.
      Officer: capture guard (item 2) + escort + flip observation below.
      ESCORT OFFICER: each own V3 ([V3] INI-confirmed) gets up to 3
      bodyguards (idle first, marching-not-fighting backfill — live
      idle pool is 0; fighting/unknown-state never yanked; fighters
      stay in the detail and are spared from follow-refresh).
      Free guards focus-fire hostiles within 15 cells of the V3
      (ESCORT_INTERCEPT, 150f cooldown); a V3 with a threat inside
      12 cells kites toward its base (ESCORT_KITE, 300f cooldown).
      GARRISON OFFICER (EXPERIMENTAL): idle own infantry within 12 cells
      of own BUNKER/NAPILL/GAPILL are ordered onto the building cell
      (vanilla enter-if-garrisonable, screen otherwise; BUNKER id
      UNCONFIRMED live — directive log prints the acted type for census).
  4. COORDINATION (mutual help, shared blackboard): Officer assigns
      first, Commander acts around it — rally skips escort bodyguards
      and threatened pullbacks (no order fights); breach position is
      published to cmdState and Escort stands down inside an active
      breach sector (15 cells / 120-frame memory), releasing guards to
      the rally with one log line. Order per scan: recon → Officer →
      Commander. One-scan lag by construction (hysteresis, no thrash).
  5. RESTART GUARD: frame-backwards detection resets scan, guard,
      officer AND blackboard state, so a new match starts clean.
  6. RADEVAC (radiation interplay, medium/hard only): while the
      radiation mod (if active) reports WARNING or ACTIVE with
      targets — read via its read-only Mod.GetStatus() — own
      infantry inside the blast cells is ordered to the nearest own
      building (WARNING evacuates preemptively with +2 margin).
      Fighting/unknown-state/exempt/civilian infantry never yanked;
      per-unit 300f cooldown + arrival hold. Silent when radiation
      is inactive or IDLE.
  7. MINER OFFICER (medium/hard only): idle war/chrono miners go
      back to work via HarvestAt (first live consumer of the
      primitive). Resume own field anchor (GetHarvestLocation),
      else share the nearest ACTIVE donor's field; docked anchors
      (at own base) don't count. Only idle miners — workers never
      yanked; SMIN/vehicles/enemies untouched. Per-miner 300f
      cooldown + arrival hold; claimed miners are skipped by rally.
  8. BUILDLAW (medium/hard only): HIGH-STAKES rows only — labs
      need WF+radar, superweapons/high-tech need the lab (Industrial
      Plant/Ore Purifier also need a refinery or Slave Miner).
      Recovery infrastructure (power/barracks/refineries/factories/
      yards/depots/radars/defenses) and modded rows deliberately NOT
      enforced — policing recovery order griefs worse than the bypass
      (see header). Sell IFF: chain broken now AND at birth
      (birth-legal never touched) AND never queue-marked fresh
      (AI.IsQueued: ordered while satisfied = queue-completion;
      marks expire QMARK_WINDOW) AND birth scan > 1 (load inventory
      assumed legal) AND past grace (1800f) AND per-type sells below
      cap 3 (then log-and-leave). Refund clawed back. AI houses only.
      MCVLAW: Allied/Soviet MCVs need WF+depot (Yuri MCV has no INI
      prerequisite — exempt); depot-less production is destroyed via
      TakeDamage (never-had rule, birth>1, mobile-only, same gates).
      Rally pool excludes MCVs/harvesters like every other layer.
  9. WAVE-SHEPHERD (medium/hard only, recall family): vanilla attack
      waves marching into prepared counters get called off instead
      of feeding free kills. A marching-not-fighting AI group
      (3+ within 12 cells) far from home (>25) facing >=3x local
      mobile strength (within 15) is ordered home with one HUD per
      wave. Fighters, unknown-state, lone units, harvesters/MCVs/
      artillery and officer-held units never touched. Transition-only
      + shared recall cooldown (300f); claims MARCH (defense tier).
  10. M3 SEED — character, adaptation, initiative (medium/hard):
      stance per house (first rushes, then alternate; hard always
      rushes and presents as Mastermind), raid escalation on
      evidence (win -> bigger next form, loss/wipe -> smaller),
      counterpunch (rusher finishes a bled-dry enemy without waiting
      for quiet), hard-only feint (one cheap unit distracts the
      visible player force while hunters work economy), grudge flips
      announced. No new forms while a breach is active (defense-first).
  11. RETARGET OFFICER (medium/hard, raid family): engaged AI combat
      redirected onto better targets without moving anyone — the
      layer for fully-engaged matches where vanilla owns every
      order. Switch iff value gap >= 1.0 and no farther than current
      engagement +2 (attack-movers commit within 15). Fighters held
      by any SmartAI layer keep SmartAI targets; harvesters/MCVs are
      never re-aimed themselves (but are priority targets: 2.0/3.0).
      Transition-only + 300f cooldown; dlog only (focus fire is the
      visible proof).

STOOD-DOWN ESCORTS are transparent to Commander drafting: a breach
release frees the guards to the rally in the same tick instead of
holding them one extra scan in assignment memory.

What it does NOT do (older versions of this file described squads,
officers, escorts, a CapabilityRegistry and framework Timer/Query/EventBus
wiring — none of that exists in main.lua; no ForceGroup, no SQUAD_NUM,
no SetSpeedPercent escort):
  - no squads, no officers/commanders, no escorts;
  - no production, spawning, or credit writes (source-verified zero
    SpawnUnit/QueueUnit/AddCredits);
  - no capture/mind-control implementation (vanilla mechanics untouched).

HOW TO ENABLE:
  1. Add smart_ai to scripts/active_mods.txt.
  2. Start a skirmish vs the AI.

WHAT HAPPENS:
  - [AI Commander - <house>] flank-breach alerts when reserves rally.
  - [AI Commander] ownership-change observations on flips.
  - Capture-risk pullback lines when a lone valuable is threatened.
