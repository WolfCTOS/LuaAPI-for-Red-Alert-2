MOD: Smart AI
ID:   smart_ai

What it does: one brain for the enemy. Reads the CapabilityRegistry and commands:
  - DEFENSE: idle enemy units intercept the best target near their base
    (empty registry = any combat units except economy/engineers).
  - OFFENSE: idle ELITE fighters strike your densest unit cluster.
  - SQUADS (merged from tactical_reassess): 3 independent squads of 3 units,
    each with its own destroy/switch-target/retreat loop + SEEK toward you.
    Settings: SQUAD_NUM/SQUAD_SIZE in main.lua. Your units are never touched.

It also owns the Squad Speed Sync ability: when the enemy AI has an
"escorted" unit (engineer or tactical officer heading to an objective), it
gathers an escort around it and matches the escort's speed to the escorted
unit (FootClass::SetSpeedPercent). The AI "thinks out loud":
"I am escorting the engineer to capture a building (oil derrick/airport...)
or repair a bridge".

HOW TO ENABLE:
  1. Enable smart_ai (add the id to scripts/active_mods.txt), Save & Apply.
  2. Start a skirmish vs the AI.

WHAT HAPPENS:
  - Idle enemy units near their base pick targets by threat-score:
    economy < MCV < vehicles < heavies (APOC/TTNK/SREF...).
  - Elite fighters attack the densest cluster (MIN_CLUSTER=2).
  - Squads recruit from AI combat units (miners/MCVs/engineers excluded),
    seek you, fight, retreat when losing, disband when wiped.
  - A heartbeat line every ~5 s in the log proves the mod is alive:
    [AI] heartbeat ... (types=registry/fallback).

LOGS: [AI], [AI-ESCORT] and [TACTICAL] messages; details in LuaAPI.log.

NOTES:
  - This mod makes the enemy MORE aggressive — the game gets harder.
  - Load order: smart_ai must see the CapabilityRegistry of other mods.
  - ESCORT: who counts as "escorted" and what the objective is.
      * Engineer: E3/ENGINEER heading to a capturable building (CAOILD —
        oil derrick, CAAIRP/NAAIRC/YAAIRC — airport, CAHOSP — hospital);
        on arrival the engineer captures it (stock RA2 behavior).
      * Officer: BORIS/TANYA heroes, else the AI's costliest unit.
        Objective: nearest enemy building as the push target.
      * Bridges: put bridge waypoint ids into BRIDGE_WAYPOINTS (main.lua)
        and the engineer will be sent there to repair (RA2 repairs on contact).
      * CAPTURE_ENEMY=false — engineer ignores enemy buildings; =true — goes.
    Config at the top of the "Squad Speed Sync capability" section in main.lua.
